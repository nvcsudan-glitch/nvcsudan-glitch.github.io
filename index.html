01Where we stand

The war stopped our sites. It did not close the company.

Most contractor profiles in Sudan today are quiet about the last three years. We would rather state ours plainly, because a client can verify it. Site operations stopped in April 2023. The legal entity, the tax file, the engineering staff and the plant were kept intact throughout, and in 2026 we returned to work.

2013
Founded in Khartoum and registered with the Ministry of Justice under partnership No. 16653.
2013 — 2023
Ten years of continuous delivery: 24 documented contracts across residential, commercial, religious and industrial work.
2023 — 2026
Site operations suspended because of the conflict in Khartoum. The company was never dissolved — registration, tax file, staff and plant were all kept intact.
2026
Back on site. Work resumed at Al-Huda, and a fit-out was completed and handed over for the Sudanese Mineral Resources Company.

05Capability

What we own is what we can promise.

A contractor's programme is only as reliable as the resources behind it. Ours are owned, inventoried and on the ground in Khartoum.

01

Owned plant & formwork

A complete formwork, scaffolding and site-plant fleet, inventoried after the suspension with no missing items. Owning it shortens mobilisation and removes the hire market from the critical path.

  • 3,000 m² slab formwork
  • 1,500 m² steel scaffolding
  • Mixers, vibrators, compactors
  • Total station & levels
02

Own materials production

A company-owned compressed-brick factory. In a market where supply is the binding constraint, producing a core material in-house protects the programme from the shortages that stall other contractors.

  • Compressed-brick factory
  • Direct agreements with steel & cement agents
03

In-house engineering team

Fourteen permanent staff: civil, architectural, electrical and mechanical engineers, a site supervisor, a stores and procurement manager and a dedicated HSE officer who reports to General Management and can stop unsafe work.

  • 14 permanent staff
  • 100+ site workers
  • Zero lost-time injuries since 2013
